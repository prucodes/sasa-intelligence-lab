## Presentation and safety boundary

The product displays this register in **Data Readiness → Catalogue**. Every visible analytical value is computed from a retained governed response. Short period series are labeled reported period history, not trend or forecast. District sources remain district grain. Configured capacity is not labeled utilization. Missing records are not converted to zero. Candidate coverage does not create an authoritative ULB identity. Gap Radar SAMPLE entities remain UNSCORED.
